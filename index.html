Quota isn't a volume problem

Reps aren't sending too little. They're sending the same next step to everyone, regardless of what the last email actually did — because reacting to a real signal today means manually unenrolling a lead, moving them to a different sequence, and rebuilding a follow-up by hand. Most reps don't have time to do that per lead, so the sequence just keeps firing on autopilot.

It's gotten worse, not better: Apple's Mail Privacy Protection has made open tracking unreliable for a large share of any list, so even the signal sequence tools do react to is now half-blind.

  • dead endBounced — flag it and move on, not another templated step to a dead address.
  • adaptOpened, no reply — the subject worked, the body didn't. Keep the subject, rewrite the body, reply in the same thread.
  • adaptClicked, no reply — relevance is proven, the ask wasn't direct enough. Send a short, direct CTA in the same thread.
  • adaptNever opened — the subject failed, the body's unproven. Full reset: new angle, new subject, new thread.
